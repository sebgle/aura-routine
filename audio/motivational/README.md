# audio/motivational/

Drop audio here — `.mp3`, `.ogg`, `.wav`, `.flac`, `.m4a`. The morning routine
shuffles through these once you press **I'M UP**, and they keep playing under
the task list until you press **FINISH**.

Or use the **upload** area on the settings page, which copies files here for you.

**Two things worth doing:**

**Normalise the volume.** Ripped clips vary wildly in loudness, and the one
thing worse than an alarm you sleep through is one that detonates at four times
the level you expected. Audacity's Normalize, or `ffmpeg -af loudnorm`.

**Use audio you have the rights to.** Your own files, purchased audiobooks,
anything you own. Clipping a segment for your own alarm clock is about as
ordinary as personal use gets.
