# audio/piano/

Drop calm instrumental audio here — `.mp3`, `.ogg`, `.wav`, `.flac`, `.m4a`.
The evening routine shuffles through these from the moment the light starts
dimming until it fades out at your bedtime.

Or use the **upload** area on the settings page.

Anything works, but the routine is built around music that can be ignored —
it plays for twenty-odd minutes while you brush your teeth and get into bed,
and ducks to silence whenever a task is announced. Something with a vocal or a
big dynamic range will pull your attention exactly when it shouldn't.
